About Me

My story, my interests, and the values that guide my professional journey.

I am Balibuza Jacob, a Congolese changemaker, social entrepreneur, graphic designer, and community outreach practitioner based in the Kakuma–Kalobeyei area of Kenya.

My work brings together creative communication, digital technology, education, community engagement, and advocacy. I believe young people and underserved communities deserve access to the knowledge, tools, and opportunities needed to shape their futures.

I contribute to initiatives focused on digital skills, youth empowerment, climate resilience, sustainable development, and inclusive innovation. I am especially interested in using design and technology to address practical challenges and amplify community voices.

I continue to develop my skills through professional experience, education, fellowships, and collaborative projects that connect local action with global goals.
